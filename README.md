## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Utah** with a passion for building things that combine engineering, software, and artificial intelligence.

I've always enjoyed understanding **how things work**. Whether it's reverse engineering a vehicle system, designing a custom 3D printed part, building an AI application, or automating repetitive tasks, I enjoy solving challenging problems by learning new skills along the way.

Rather than following tutorials from start to finish, I prefer starting with an idea that's just beyond what I know today and figuring it out as I go. Every project becomes an opportunity to learn something new.
